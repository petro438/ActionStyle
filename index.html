Style guide

The rules that keep us consistent

Draft · confirm items in brackets

Odds & lines

DOAmerican odds with a sign: +150, -110 DOSpread with the price: Bills +3.5 (-110) DOTotals as Over 47.5 / Under 47.5 in prose, O 47.5 in tables DON’TMix formats in one article, or drop the sign: 150 DON’TQuote a line without the book and the time it was pulled

Names & brands

  • Sportsbooks as they style themselves: DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars Sportsbook, bet365
  • Teams by nickname after first reference [confirm: city on first reference?]
  • Our product is Action Network PRO, then PRO
  • Experts by full name on first reference, last name after

Picks

  • Every pick uses the Pick box, never bold text alone
  • Include units and a “bet to” number when the expert gives one
  • Show the reasoning before the pick box, not only after it
  • The CTA under a pick goes to the book whose price you quoted

Times, numbers & compliance

  • Eastern time, written 8:20 p.m. ET
  • Percentages as numerals: 62%
  • Records as 12-4 ATS, 8-2 to the Over
  • Responsible gaming line on every story with a sportsbook CTA [exact text from compliance]

Images

  • Every story gets a featured image, from [Getty / Imagn / AP] or our templates in [Canva / Figma]
  • Featured images at least 1200px wide, 16:9, so they qualify for large previews in Google Discover
  • Alt text says what’s in the photo, not the headline
  • Credit every photo in the caption

Internal links

  • [3–5] internal links per article, the first in the first [3] paragraphs
  • Link to at least one money page (promo or review) when the story mentions a book
  • Descriptive anchors: “Chiefs vs. Bills odds,” never “click here”
  • Priority pages to link this week: [link to list]

Before you hit publish

  • Featured image set, credited, with alt text
  • Every table checked in Mobile view
  • All CTAs inserted from the CMS block, none hand-built
  • No new Infogram embeds
  • Internal links in, including a money page
  • After publish: open the live page on your phone and check that images and tables rendered
